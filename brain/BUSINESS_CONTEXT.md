# Business Context

Reach International is an Indian industrial equipment company providing aerial work platforms (AWPs), access equipment, and material handling equipment (MHE).

**Production domain:** https://www.reachinternational.co.in/
**Tagline:** Reaching All Heights
**Locale:** en-IN
